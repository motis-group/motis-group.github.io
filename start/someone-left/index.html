The person who knew how it worked has gone.
 
Nobody wrote the rules down, because for years nobody needed to. They lived in one person’s head and they worked fine there.
Now whoever picked it up is reconstructing those rules from memory every time they do the job. They get most of them right. The ones they get wrong don’t announce themselves, so they surface months later as a record nobody can find, a figure that doesn’t reconcile, someone asking a question you can’t answer.
You can feel this happening. Naming it is the hard part, because it doesn’t look like a broken system. It looks like the new person being slow.
 
I read the system cold, find where the written version and the working version have come apart, and put the rules somewhere the software can enforce them instead of a person having to remember.
 
If they are still serving notice, tell me now. Extraction while they are in the building is a different and cheaper job than reconstruction after they have gone.
