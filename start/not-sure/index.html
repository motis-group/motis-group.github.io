You don’t quite believe your own reporting.
 
Nothing’s on fire. Two reports disagree by a bit, someone quietly reconciles them before the meeting, and nobody’s sure which one was right. It’s been that way long enough to feel normal.
 
The honest answer is that there’s probably nothing here urgent enough to pay me for yet. Problems like this get fixed when something forces them, and nothing is forcing this. If I took your money today I’d be selling you a fix for a problem you’ve already adapted to.
 
So take the method instead. This is how I read a system I’ve never seen and work out where it’s lying to you. Twenty minutes, no login, and you can run it on your own setup this afternoon.
 
⊹ list every page and product the public site will give you, before you log in to anything
⊹ count the things that should agree and don’t: totals, categories, anything with the word “all” in its name
⊹ look for duplicates with version words in them: new, v2, final, copy
⊹ find the field that is supposed to sort everything, and check how many records have it empty
⊹ ask who would notice if each of those was wrong for a week
 
If someone leaves, or it breaks where a customer can see it, that’s when to call.
